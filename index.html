How we work

Boring on purpose.

No theatrics. Most engagements follow the same arc, whether it's an audit or a six-month embed.

01 · Week 1

Listen first

We sit in on your standups, read your backlog, and talk to two or three users. Most "AI problems" turn out to be product problems wearing a hat.

02 · Week 2

Pick the smallest thing

One feature, one user, one workflow. We pick the highest-value thing your team can ship in the engagement window and write it up properly.

03 · Weeks 3–5

Ship it

Specs, design partner check-ins, sprint reviews. We work alongside your engineers, not above them. We ship to production, not a demo.

04 · Final week

Hand off

You get a written playbook for how to continue without us. If we keep going, we keep going. If we don't, you're not stranded.
