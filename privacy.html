Privacy Policy

Effective: May 19, 2026 · Last updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Anvil Studio ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit anvilstudio.io, book a call with us, or otherwise interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company name and role
  • Message content (e.g., what you tell us during inquiry or booking)
  • Booking and scheduling information
  • Any other information you voluntarily submit through our forms or communications

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit the site:

  • Analytics data (pages visited, time on page, referring source)
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and general location
  • Cookie data (see our Cookie Notice)

2. Why We Collect It

  • To respond to your inquiries and book calls
  • To provide our services and deliverables
  • To improve our site and content
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues
  • To send service-related communications
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Who Processes Your Data

We use a small number of trusted third-party tools to operate our business:

  • Booking tools (e.g., scheduling software) to manage intro calls
  • Email tools (e.g., Google Workspace) to communicate with you
  • Analytics providers to understand site usage
  • Hosting providers (e.g., GitHub Pages, Cloudflare) to deliver the site
  • Client-approved vendors introduced during engagements, only as needed

Each provider is contractually required to handle data responsibly under their own privacy terms.

4. We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

Anvil Studio does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. You may also ask us privacy-related questions. Email us at hello@anvilstudio.io and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Children's Privacy

Anvil Studio is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Inquiry and booking data is typically retained for up to 24 months unless required longer by law.

8. International Users

Our services are operated from the United States. If you access the site from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@anvilstudio.io.
