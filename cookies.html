Cookie Notice

This notice explains how Anvil Studio uses cookies and similar technologies on anvilstudio.io.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are commonly used to make sites work, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use them.

2. How We Use Them

We may use cookies, analytics, and third-party tools to:

  • Understand site traffic (which pages are visited, how visitors arrive)
  • Improve the site's content, structure, and performance
  • Ensure the site loads correctly across devices and browsers
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or security issues

3. Types of Cookies

  • Essential cookies: Required for the site to function (e.g., basic navigation, security). These cannot be disabled without breaking site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand site usage in aggregate. These do not identify you personally.
  • Third-party cookies: Set by services we use (e.g., booking tools, hosting providers, analytics).

4. Disabling Cookies

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Receive a prompt before cookies are stored

Note that disabling some cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Future Tracking and Pixels

If we add non-essential tracking technologies in the future (such as marketing pixels, retargeting tags, or advertising cookies), we will display a consent banner before those tools run, and you will be able to accept or decline.

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no industry standard for how to interpret this signal, we currently do not respond to it. We do not engage in cross-site behavioral advertising that would commonly be associated with DNT signals.

7. Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
